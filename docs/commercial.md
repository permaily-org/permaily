The [Permaily Personal Use License](../license.md) lets anyone, organisations included, inspect and verify the software, and only lets individual people run it, for themselves. Any other use needs a commercial license, including:

- running it, or any use beyond inspecting and verifying it, by or for a company, nonprofit, school, university or government body;
- hosting Permaily, or a service based on it, for other people, paid or free;
- distributing modified versions;
- internal business use.

Inspecting the code, building it to verify it, verifying the public Permaily service and publishing what you find need no other license, whether you are an individual or an organisation, such as a security firm, a newspaper or a university (license.md 1.2, section 2). Running the code stays for an individual, for their own personal use; an organisation that wants to run it, or to do anything beyond checking it, needs a commercial license.

Commercial licenses are granted by the Licensor (Leynier Gutiérrez González and Tony Raúl Blanco Fernández). To request one, open an issue titled "Commercial license" in the repository or write to the owners listed on the GitHub organization page. Pricing is set per agreement.

A licensee that self-hosts runs the service itself, so no data processing agreement is needed with us. The hosted service is not offered to organisations yet; before it is, [permaily.com/data-processing](https://permaily.com/data-processing/) will say what the data processing agreement covers.
